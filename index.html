1. About This Site

Kanjirowa Mart is an online store serving customers across Nepal. These Terms explain what to expect when you create an account or place an order with us.

2. Accounts

  • You must provide a name, a real email address, and a phone number to register, and verify the email address with a 6-digit code before the account is created.
  • One email address may be used for one account. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential.
  • You must be logged in to add items to your cart or check out.

3. Orders & Payment

  • Selecting "Card" or "Scan to Pay" confirms your order on-screen once your payment details are entered.
  • Card numbers you type are not verified for validity and are never stored in full; only the last 4 digits are kept with your order record so you and the store admin can identify which payment was used.
  • Prices, discounts, and stock levels shown are updated periodically and are subject to change without notice.

4. Delivery

A flat delivery fee of Rs. 100 is added to every order. Delivery timelines shown elsewhere on the site are estimates and may vary by location.

5. Returns & Support

For any question about an order, contact aimloqris@gmail.com with your order ID.

1. What We Collect

  • Account info: name, email, phone number, and password, entered when you sign up.
  • Order info: delivery name, phone, address/city, items purchased, and — for card payments — only the last 4 digits typed (never the full number or CVV).
  • Reviews: any name, location, and text you submit with a product review.

2. Where It's Stored

When the shared store backend is available, account and order information is stored in the store database so it can be used across devices. When the backend is unavailable and the site is running in standalone/demo mode, the site falls back to browser storage on that device.

3. Third-Party Services Used

  • EmailJS — sends the 6-digit email verification code to the address you sign up with.
  • FormSubmit — relays a new-order notification to the store's own inbox.
  • QR Server API — generates the scan-to-pay QR image shown at checkout.
  • Unsplash — hosts the product photos shown in the catalog.

Only the minimum data needed for each of those specific purposes (e.g. your email + verification code) is sent to that service; nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

4. How Your Info Is Used

Solely to run the store experience: creating your account, verifying your email, processing orders, showing your own order history, and — if you are the designated store admin — showing aggregated orders/users in the admin dashboard.

5. Your Choices

You can log out, delete your browser's local storage for this site, or clear individual entries via your browser's developer tools at any time.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too.
